# Estimate My Mortgage privacy policy

Updated 7 October 2026.

Estimate My Mortgage stores estimate names, property addresses and financial assumptions on your device using Apple's Core Data. The app has no account, advertising, analytics SDK or developer-operated backend. Shreyas Sane does not receive your saved estimates. Your device's backups may include the app's local data.

Address suggestions and property maps use Apple's MapKit service. When you choose to find an address or show its map, the requested address or search text is sent to Apple to provide that feature. The app does not request access to your device's current location. Manual address entry and mortgage calculations work without using the map features. Apple's services are subject to [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

Sharing is optional. The system share sheet sends the estimate summary to the destination you choose. Review that destination's privacy practices before sharing personal information. Deleting an estimate removes it from the app's active local store; existing device backups may still contain earlier data.

This policy describes version 2.0. Future changes will be reflected here and in the app's About & Privacy screen. For app questions, use the [repository's issue tracker](https://github.com/Shreyasdbz/estimate-my-mortgage/issues); avoid including private addresses or financial details in public issues.
